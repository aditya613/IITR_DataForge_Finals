# WORLD HISTORY REFERENCE DOCUMENT
# Authoritative Source for Historical Fact Verification

## Section 1: Ancient Civilizations

The Great Pyramid of Giza was built around 2560 BCE for Pharaoh Khufu. It is the oldest and largest of the three pyramids in the Giza pyramid complex and was the tallest man-made structure for over 3,800 years.

The Roman Empire fell in 476 CE when Romulus Augustulus was deposed by the Germanic leader Odoacer. This date marks the traditional end of the Western Roman Empire.

The ancient city of Rome at its peak had a population of approximately 1 million people, making it the largest city in the ancient world.

## Section 2: Medieval Period

The Black Death killed approximately 75-200 million people between 1346 and 1353. It was one of the most devastating pandemics in human history, killing an estimated 30-60% of Europe's population.

The Magna Carta was signed in 1215 CE by King John of England. This document established the principle that everyone, including the king, was subject to the law.

The Crusades were a series of religious wars between 1095 and 1291 CE, primarily aimed at recovering the Holy Land from Islamic rule.

## Section 3: World Wars

World War I lasted from 1914 to 1918 and resulted in approximately 17 million deaths. The war was triggered by the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria on June 28, 1914.

World War II lasted from 1939 to 1945 and resulted in approximately 70-85 million deaths. It was the deadliest conflict in human history.

D-Day occurred on June 6, 1944, when Allied forces invaded Normandy, France. It was the largest seaborne invasion in history, involving approximately 156,000 troops.

The atomic bombs were dropped on Hiroshima on August 6, 1945, and Nagasaki on August 9, 1945, leading to Japan's surrender and the end of World War II.

## Section 4: Space Exploration

The first human in space was Yuri Gagarin on April 12, 1961. The Soviet cosmonaut orbited Earth once aboard the Vostok 1 spacecraft.

Neil Armstrong became the first person to walk on the Moon on July 20, 1969. He was part of the Apollo 11 mission, which launched from Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

The famous words spoken were "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

The Space Shuttle program operated from 1981 to 2011, completing 135 missions. It was the first reusable spacecraft system.

## Section 5: Modern History

The Berlin Wall fell on November 9, 1989. The wall had divided East and West Berlin since 1961 during the Cold War.

The Soviet Union dissolved on December 26, 1991. This event marked the end of the Cold War and resulted in 15 independent republics.

The European Union was formally established by the Maastricht Treaty, which entered into force on November 1, 1993.

The World Wide Web was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 at CERN. The first website went live on August 6, 1991.

## Section 6: American History

The Declaration of Independence was signed on July 4, 1776. Thomas Jefferson was the primary author of this founding document.

The American Civil War lasted from 1861 to 1865. It resulted in approximately 620,000 soldier deaths and the abolition of slavery.

The United States Constitution was ratified on June 21, 1788. The Bill of Rights, comprising the first ten amendments, was ratified on December 15, 1791.
