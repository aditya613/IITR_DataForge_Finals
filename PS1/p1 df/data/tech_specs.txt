# TECHNOLOGY SPECIFICATIONS DOCUMENT
# Authoritative Source for AI and Computing Fact Verification

## Section 1: Large Language Models

GPT-4 was released in March 2023 by OpenAI. It represents a significant advancement in artificial intelligence capabilities and uses a transformer architecture.

The model has an estimated 1.7 trillion parameters, making it one of the largest language models ever created. GPT-4 is a multimodal model capable of processing both text and images.

Claude 3 Opus was released by Anthropic in March 2024 and demonstrates strong reasoning capabilities.

## Section 2: Quantum Computing

IBM's Condor processor achieved 1,121 qubits in December 2023. This represents a major milestone in quantum computing hardware development.

Google's Sycamore processor has 53 qubits and demonstrated quantum supremacy in 2019. The Sycamore processor performed a calculation in 200 seconds that would take classical supercomputers approximately 10,000 years.

Quantum computers use quantum bits (qubits) that can exist in superposition states, enabling certain calculations to be performed much faster than classical computers.

## Section 3: GPU Specifications

NVIDIA H100 GPU has 80GB of HBM3 memory and delivers 3,958 TFLOPS of FP8 performance. It is designed specifically for AI training and inference workloads.

The H100 uses TSMC's 4nm process node. The Tensor Core technology enables accelerated matrix operations crucial for deep learning.

NVIDIA A100 GPU, the predecessor to H100, has 40GB or 80GB HBM2e memory variants.

## Section 4: Battery Technology

Lithium-ion batteries have an energy density of approximately 250-300 Wh/kg. They are the dominant technology for electric vehicles and consumer electronics.

Solid-state batteries promise energy densities exceeding 400 Wh/kg. These batteries use solid electrolytes instead of liquid, potentially offering improved safety and energy density.

Sodium-ion batteries are emerging as a potential alternative to lithium-ion for grid-scale storage applications.

## Section 5: Internet Infrastructure

5G networks offer peak speeds up to 20 Gbps with latency as low as 1 millisecond. 5G uses millimeter wave spectrum for high-speed applications.

Starlink satellites orbit at approximately 550 km altitude. The constellation aims to provide global broadband internet coverage through thousands of low-Earth orbit satellites.

Fiber optic cables can transmit data at speeds exceeding 100 Tbps using wavelength division multiplexing technology.

## Section 6: Semiconductor Manufacturing

TSMC produces chips at the 3nm process node as of 2024. TSMC is the world's largest semiconductor foundry and manufactures chips for Apple, AMD, and NVIDIA.

EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet) lithography uses 13.5nm wavelength light for chip manufacturing. EUV technology enables the creation of smaller and more efficient transistors.

Intel is developing its Intel 18A process node, targeting 1.8nm equivalent density. Samsung also produces chips at advanced nodes competitive with TSMC.
